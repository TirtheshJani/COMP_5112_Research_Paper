## AI policy and course rules

AI help is allowed, but the student owns every error submitted, including hallucinated citations and wrong proofs. Uploading the PDF of a reference is the check that it exists. AI use must be disclosed in the poster, the presentation and the final paper. For Assignment 3, AI could help understand a paper but not write the summaries. BibTeX is meant to come from Google Scholar exports (the paper's entries were checked against Crossref and arXiv instead; an open item to raise with Aaron).

## Textbook

Booth, Colomb, Williams, Bizup and FitzGerald, The Craft of Research, 5th edition (University of Chicago Press, 2024). The PDF in the Project is the 4th edition; the EPUB is the 5th. Use 5th-edition sections: 1.3 (so what?), 2.1 to 2.3 (problem as condition plus consequence; pure versus applied; the fourth step for the practical application), 2.4 (check the gap is real), 3.4.2 (source reliability), 4.3 (reading for a problem), 9.4 (what to acknowledge), chapters 10 to 14 for structure (14.1 to 14.4 introduction as context, problem, response; 14.7 call for research), chapter 16 (presentations). Vocabulary used throughout: research interest, topic, question, problem.
