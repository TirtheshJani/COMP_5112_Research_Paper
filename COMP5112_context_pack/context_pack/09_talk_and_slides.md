## The talk

12 minutes at the course conference, 30 November or 7 December. `research/paper/talk/talk_plan.md` predates the reviews (it uses 81, 2.25, "6 to 16" and "280 exact"); the deck replaces it. Course guidance for talks: Craft of Research chapter 16; Aaron's poster-era advice (main point first, simple before complex, callbacks, check in with the audience, have things to say that are not on the slides).
